Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Is Crossio safe and ban-free on Android?
Yes, Crossio is completely safe. It operates strictly as a passive system window overlay utilizing standard Android display APIs. It does not inspect, inject, intercept, or modify any third-party game binaries or system memory.

How do I customize crosshair size, color, and opacity?
Launch Crossio on your mobile device, pick any crosshair design from the extensive community gallery, and use intuitive sliders to adjust line thickness, outer radius, center gap, and opacity in real time with live screen preview.

Does Crossio impact mobile gaming battery life or FPS?
Crossio is designed with ultra-low power consumption in mind. It uses lightweight canvas rendering with minimal CPU and RAM cycles, ensuring zero latency, zero frame drops, and maximum performance during your gaming sessions.
